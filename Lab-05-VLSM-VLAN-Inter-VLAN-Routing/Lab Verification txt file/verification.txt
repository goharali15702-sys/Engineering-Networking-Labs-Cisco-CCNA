Lab 05 Verification
VLSM + VLANs + Inter-VLAN Routing

1. Switch VLAN Verification

Command:

show vlan brief

Expected VLANs:

VLAN 10 — HR
VLAN 20 — IT
VLAN 30 — SALES
VLAN 40 — MANAGEMENT

2. Trunk Verification

Command:

show interfaces trunk

Trunk Port:

Fa0/5

Fa0/5 successfully operates as the trunk between the switch and router.

3. Router Interface Verification

Command:

show ip interface brief

Router subinterfaces:

G0/0.10 — 192.168.100.1
G0/0.20 — 192.168.100.65
G0/0.30 — 192.168.100.97
G0/0.40 — 192.168.100.113

All required subinterfaces are operational.

4. Routing Table Verification

Command:

show ip route

Connected networks:

192.168.100.0/26
192.168.100.64/27
192.168.100.96/28
192.168.100.112/30

5. Inter-VLAN Connectivity

From the HR PC:

ping 192.168.100.66
ping 192.168.100.98
ping 192.168.100.114

Result:

Successful communication between devices in different VLANs.

6. Final Result

VLSM addressing was successfully implemented.

Four VLANs were configured.

The switch-to-router link was configured as a trunk on Fa0/5.

Router-on-a-Stick was successfully configured.

Inter-VLAN routing and end-to-end connectivity were verified successfully.
